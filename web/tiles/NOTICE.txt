Rando city tiles (cities.pmtiles, and the region archives listed in
regions.json: kr-seoul, kr-capital-w/-s/-e/-n, kr-busan .pmtiles) — data
credits and licence

These archives are derivative databases of:

- OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database License 1.0
  (ODbL). https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Overture Maps Foundation, buildings + transportation themes, release
  2026-10-21.0, ODbL 1.0. https://overturemaps.org — which in Korea also
  carry footprints from Qian Shi et al., East Asian building footprints
  (Zenodo, doi:10.5281/zenodo.8174931, CC BY 4.0), and in Canada Microsoft
  Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL / CDLA-Permissive-2.0). Roads also
  include data from TomTom.
- In Korea, building heights and floor counts from the Ministry of Land,
  Infrastructure and Transport's building register through VWorld
  (국토교통부 GIS건물통합정보 / 도로명주소 건물, 공공누리 제1유형 — attribution):
  본 저작물은 국토교통부에서 작성하여 공공누리 제1유형으로 개방한 건물 정보를
  이용하였으며, 해당 저작물은 국토교통부 브이월드(https://www.vworld.kr)에서
  무료로 다운받으실 수 있습니다.
- Itaewon only: measured building heights from Google Photorealistic 3D
  Tiles, used by the owner's decision (see docs/map_data.md).

Modifications: footprints simplified, heights estimated where the sources
have none (type, area, neighbours), roads clipped into 1/4-tile chunks.

The archives are published under ODbL 1.0 and are downloadable as they are
from https://randoirl.vercel.app/tiles/cities.pmtiles and
https://randoirl.vercel.app/tiles/<file> for each file named in
https://randoirl.vercel.app/tiles/regions.json. Anywhere they don't
cover, the app reads OpenFreeMap's worldwide tiles
(https://openfreemap.org, © OpenMapTiles https://openmaptiles.org/, data
from OpenStreetMap).
