# Rando wordmark — first pass

Single typeface, single weight per option. Tight tracking. Black on transparent. Each option ships as an OUTLINED svg (exact, font-independent) and a LIVE-text twin (editable — needs the font installed).

| # | Treatment | Files | Why | Width (cap-heights) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Century Gothic Regular · lowercase · geometric | `A-geometric-lower.svg`, `A-geometric-lower-live.svg` | Perfect circles in a, d, o echo the round head of the icon. Lowercase keeps it soft and approachable — the app is about meeting people, not a brand shouting. Weakest at 16 px: thin strokes vanish. | 3.97 |
| B | Segoe UI Semibold · Title case · humanist sans | `B-humanist-title.svg`, `B-humanist-title-live.svg` | The closest thing on this machine to the SF-Pro restraint of the brief. Title case reads as a product name on an app-store page. Semibold survives the badge sizes; R–a tightened so the cap doesn't float off the word. | 3.89 |
| C | Bahnschrift SemiBold (DIN) · caps · tight | `C-din-caps.svg`, `C-din-caps-live.svg` | All caps, engineered DIN forms — the most legible at 16–24 px of the four, and the most 'signage' in feel (fits a city/venue product). Least warm; pairs with the icon for the softness. | 4.57 |
| D | Franklin Gothic Demi · lowercase · grotesque | `D-grotesque-lower.svg`, `D-grotesque-lower-live.svg` | Heavy, compact, confident. Holds its weight in a 32 px badge better than A or B; the double-storey a gives it more character than the geometric option. Slight risk of feeling 'sports/retail' rather than 'Apple-quiet'. | 3.34 |

`sizes.png` shows each at display size and at 48 / 24 / 16 px font size (the 16 px row approximates a favicon-adjacent label — the icon alone carries the favicon).
